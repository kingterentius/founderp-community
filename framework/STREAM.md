# The STREAM Framework

*Six resources every decision draws on.*

Most planning systems manage one resource: time. But a founder running more than one thing at once — a business, a nonprofit, a household, a creative practice — runs out of other things first. Attention. Energy. Goodwill with the people who matter. Cash.

STREAM names all six, so you can manage them together instead of discovering, too late, which one you overspent.

| | Resource | The question it asks |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | **Space** | Where does your work happen — physically and digitally — and does that place help or hinder it? |
| **T** | **Time** | Where did your hours actually go, and did that match the plan? |
| **R** | **Relationships** | Which relationships are you investing in, and which are you drawing down? |
| **E** | **Energy** | What fills your capacity to do the work, and what drains it? |
| **A** | **Attention** | What are you focused on? Often the scarcest resource, and the first to disappear when you run several things at once. |
| **M** | **Money** | What comes in, what goes out, and what is it for? |

## How to use it

STREAM works at three speeds.

### 1. Daily pulse — 30 seconds
Each morning, rate each resource from **1 to 5**. Circle the one demanding the most attention today.

### 2. Every hour, tagged
Code each block of time to the part of your life or business it served (see [OPERATING-MODEL.md](OPERATING-MODEL.md)). Over a week, you see where your resources actually went — not where you hoped they would.

### 3. Weekly review — 10 minutes
Score each resource from **1 to 10** and name the main driver behind each score. The two or three lowest tell you what next week has to protect.

## Where STREAM fits

STREAM is the resource layer. Above it sit three pillars that describe what the resources are *for* — see [LGS.md](LGS.md). Every hour spends some mix of the six resources on one of the three pillars. Put the two together and you can answer the only planning question that matters:

> **Is the way I'm spending myself building the life and work I actually want?**
