# Live / Give / Save

*Three pillars that describe what your resources are for.*

STREAM tells you **what** you're spending. Live / Give / Save tells you **on what**.

## Live — the life that powers the work
| Area | Covers |
|---|---|
| Wellness | Body, mind, rest |
| Education | What you're learning |
| Challenges | Skill trials, rites of passage, physical feats, facing what you avoid |
| Fun | Play and joy |
| Transportation | Getting where you need to be |
| Security | Home, safety, protection |
| Food | What fuels you |

## Give — what you put into the world
| Area | Covers |
|---|---|
| Identity | Your public self — brand, values, reputation |
| Career | The work that pays |
| Creativity | Making things |
| Writing | Your written body of work |
| Philanthropy | Giving money, time, and resources; programs; service |

## Save — what you protect and build up
| Area | Covers |
|---|---|
| Budget | The money plan |
| People | Partner, network, community, family, mentors |
| Environment | The places you live and work |
| Technology | Your tools and systems |
| Waste | What to cut |

## Using the pillars

- **Balance is not the goal; awareness is.** A launch week will be mostly Give. A recovery week should be mostly Live. The point is to choose it, not to discover it afterwards.
- **One home per thing.** Every project, task, and calendar block belongs to exactly one area. If it seems to belong to two, pick the one it serves *first*.
