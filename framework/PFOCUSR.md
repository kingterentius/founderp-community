# PFOCUSR — one job per day

*A weekly rhythm that gives every day a single theme.*

| Day | Letter | Theme | The day's job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunday | **P** | Planning | Look back, look ahead, set the week |
| Monday | **F** | Fulfillment | Deliver what's promised |
| Tuesday | **O** | Offers | Proposals, pitches, outreach |
| Wednesday | **C** | Content | Writing and creating |
| Thursday | **U** | Urgent | What can't slip; governance |
| Friday | **S** | Strategy | Think above the day-to-day; partnerships |
| Saturday | **R** | Review | Reconcile the week; rest |

## How it works with STREAM

- **Blocked time is for commitments only** — meetings and anything that needs you present. Everything else happens organically against the day's theme.
- **Log after, not before.** At the end of each day, record where the time actually went (see [OPERATING-MODEL.md](OPERATING-MODEL.md)). Saturday's Review reads those logs; Sunday's Planning uses them.
